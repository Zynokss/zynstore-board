# Getting started

## For shoppers

1. Open the [catalog](https://store.zynoks.com/catalog). Search or filter the current items.
2. Open a product and check its price, size, color and availability.
3. Add the item to your cart. Review quantities and options before checkout.
4. Enter the required contact and delivery details. Check the final total and payment instructions shown by the store.
5. Use cash on delivery or, when offered, the configured bank transfer option. A recorded order is not proof that a transfer has been received.
6. Keep the order reference privately. Open [order tracking](https://store.zynoks.com/track-order) or the account screen to check recorded progress.

Account screens support viewing orders and saving address information. Do not assume password recovery or email confirmation is available without confirming it with the operator.

## For store operators

1. Obtain an authorized account from the owner and sign in at [ZynBoard](https://eboard.zynoks.com).
2. Review Dashboard and Analytics for recorded order summaries. These are not audited financial or traffic reports.
3. Use Products Table or Catalog Grid to manage products. Changes affect the connected store.
4. Use Orders Feed to review orders and update status. CSV exports contain customer information and must be stored privately.
5. Sign out after use on a shared device.

Do not depend on the manual order form, Team Access, local coupon settings or support-ticket confirmation for a server-backed workflow. See the [FAQ](faq.md) and [backup guidance](backup-and-restore.md).

For help, contact contact@zynoks.com. Never place customer or payment information in public issues.
